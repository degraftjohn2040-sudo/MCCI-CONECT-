# Privacy

Church membership and pastoral information can be sensitive. Do not collect real data with this prototype until the church has approved a lawful basis, notice, access controls, retention, incident process and applicable legal review. Collect the minimum needed; obtain and record appropriate consent where required. Do not place prayer requests, pastoral notes, health details, or minors' information into general member or announcement fields.

The current member record contains name, optional phone/email, and creation time. All authorized member readers can see the entire directory; there is no scope or consent enforcement. Backups include all records and session material. No third-party data sharing is implemented. No deletion/export/correction interface, automated retention or data subject request process exists. Access, audit, storage encryption and recovery need further work. Design future messaging and AI features to require explicit authorization and prevent unnecessary disclosure.
